Nas Social Manager

Nas Social Manager is a personal social-media automation tool that helps an authorized account owner prepare and publish original short-form video content to connected platforms.

What it does

The tool can prepare short-form videos, generate titles/descriptions/hashtags, and upload content to social-media accounts only after the account owner has connected and authorized those accounts.

This site is provided for the Nas Social Manager app and its connected services.
