Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Information the app may process

When you connect a social-media account, Nas Social Manager may receive account identifiers, authorization tokens, and limited profile or posting information required to authenticate the account and perform actions you approve.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the app, authenticate connected accounts, prepare content, upload or publish content, and maintain basic workflow status.

Tokens and credentials

Authorization tokens and API credentials are intended to be stored as private secrets and are not displayed publicly. Users should never place secret tokens or API keys in public repositories.

Sharing

Personal information is not sold to advertisers. Information may be transmitted to third-party services only as needed to perform the requested functionality, including TikTok, YouTube, GitHub, Pexels, or other services the user chooses to connect.

Retention

Authentication tokens may remain stored until the user removes them, revokes access, deletes the app configuration, or the token expires. Generated media and workflow logs may be retained by the services used to run the automation according to their own retention policies.

User control

You can revoke connected-account access from the relevant platform's account settings and remove stored secrets from the automation environment at any time.

Security

Reasonable steps are taken to keep credentials private, including storing them as secrets rather than in public source code. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Nas Social Manager is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the account or repository associated with this app.
